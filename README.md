<section id="work"><div class="w">
<div class="hd"><span class="k">Featured work</span><h2>Engineering built around a real constraint.</h2><p>My featured project starts with a real problem, the delay between collecting data and acting on it, and ends with a system built to remove it.</p></div>

<article class="case"><div class="side"><div><small>AERIAL AI · REVA University · 13 June 2026</small><h3>Advanced Aerial Anomaly Analysis and 3D Reconstruction System</h3><p>A drone that reconstructs the world in 3D and flags anomalies while it is still flying.</p></div><div class="tg"><span>MATLAB</span><span>Simulink</span><span>Deep learning</span><span>Edge compute</span></div></div>
<div class="body">
<div><h4>THE PROBLEM</h4><p>Most aerial inspection records data first and processes it later. That gap between acquisition and insight means problems are found after the drone has already landed.</p></div>
<div><h4>THE APPROACH</h4><ul><li>Moved computation to the edge so the drone processes data in the air rather than storing it for later.</li><li>Integrated real-time aerial 3D reconstruction with deep-learning-based computer vision.</li><li>Designed the pipeline around high GPU inference-speed requirements.</li><li>Processed spatial and visual data at the same time during flight.</li></ul></div>
<div><h4>THE OUTCOME</h4><p>The delay between data acquisition and actionable insight is removed. Reconstruction and anomaly analysis happen during the flight itself.</p></div></div></article>




</div></section>

<section id="pipeline"><div class="w">
<div class="hd"><span class="k">Inside the aerial system</span><h2>How it works, step by step.</h2><p>The idea behind my aerial project: do the whole job during the flight, so there is no wait between collecting data and acting on it.</p></div>
<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><b>1</b><h3>Capture</h3><p>The drone collects spatial and visual data of the structure or area as it flies.</p></div>
<div class="step"><b>2</b><h3>Process at the edge</h3><p>Instead of storing data for later, computation happens on the drone itself, close to where the data is created.</p></div>
<div class="step"><b>3</b><h3>Reconstruct in 3D</h3><p>Spatial and visual data are processed together in the air to build a 3D model in real time.</p></div>
<div class="step"><b>4</b><h3>Detect anomalies</h3><p>Deep-learning computer vision analyses the scene during flight, which needs fast GPU inference to keep up.</p></div>
<div class="step"><b>5</b><h3>Act on it</h3><p>The delay between data acquisition and actionable insight is removed, so findings are ready as the flight happens.</p></div>
</div>
<p style="margin-top:22px;color:var(--mut)">Built and modelled with MATLAB and Simulink at REVA University (13 June 2026).</p>
</div></section>
